Method and gates
  • Correct answer: when agent and team agree, their shared decision; when they disagree, the expert's verdict (agent wrong / team wrong / both acceptable / both wrong with the correct decision). Unreviewed disagreements assume the team was right.
  • Matching: action exact; payout within 5.0% or 50.
  • GO gate: accuracy lower 95% bound ≥ 90%, critical rate ≤ 2.0%, cost ≤ 60 per 100, at least 30 cases.
  • Guardrail: lowest confidence threshold where the auto-handled slice passes the GO gate and covers ≥ 30% of cases.
  • Spot checks: 5% of agreements are sent to experts to estimate shared mistakes.
  • Audit: every case, decision and review is recorded in a hash-chained log; shadow verify-audit detects any edit or deletion.
