How it works

Shadow

The agent decides every case the team decides, from past records or live traffic. Nothing is executed.

Compare

Field-by-field matching with tolerances. Every disagreement is weighted by what that mistake would cost.

Adjudicate

A blind AI reviewer suggests who was right; an expert confirms. A sample of agreements is spot-checked too.

Decide

Each segment gets GO, GO WITH GUARDRAILS or NO-GO, using confidence intervals, with confidence-threshold guardrails.
